Why a spine and not a directory

Directories rot. A spine holds. Everything financial in Nigeria eventually settles through one of a handful of rails or answers to one licence class. Map those first and every vendor, policy and service has somewhere to attach.

Licence status is a moving fact. CBN has revoked licences of multiple payment service providers. Treat every licence claim in this market — including any you read here — as valid only for the date it was checked, and confirm with the regulator before relying on it.
